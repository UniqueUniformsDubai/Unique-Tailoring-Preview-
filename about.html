Who we are

The Unique approach: personal service, professional standards.

Unique Uniforms & Tailoring brings together two complementary services: corporate uniforms and staff fitting solutions for businesses, and thoughtful alterations and tailoring for individuals.

We believe that the right fit makes a difference — in how a team represents its company and in how someone feels wearing their favourite clothes.

Established in Dubai in 2021

Our story — born from a challenge, built with purpose.

Unique Tailoring Dubai began in 2021, during the uncertainty of the COVID-19 period. After losing his finance management role at a leading regional business services company, our founder faced a defining decision at the end of two decades of employment.

He could return to his home country with his family, leaving behind their established life in Dubai, including the children’s schooling. Or he could take a risk, start something new and build a business of his own.

He chose to build something Unique.

That decision became Unique Tailoring Dubai: a new chapter shaped by resilience, responsibility and a commitment to serving individuals and businesses through tailoring, alterations and corporate uniform solutions.
