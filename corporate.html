Ordering information

Flexible orders, practical minimum quantities.

We welcome enquiries from businesses of all sizes. For custom uniform production, special fabric sourcing, embroidery and logo printing, minimum quantities generally start from approximately 25 pieces or sets, depending on the design, fabric and production requirements.

Smaller orders are considered individually. Where suitable materials are already available, or customers provide their own fabric, we may be able to accommodate lower quantities. Our aim is to recommend the most practical and cost-effective solution while maintaining quality.

Fabric & branding

For larger corporate orders, we can arrange suitable fabrics, colour coordination, embroidery and logo printing. Minimum quantities depend on the material and setup requirements.

Production timeframe

Custom uniform production generally takes approximately 1–6 weeks, depending on quantities, design, fabric availability and branding. We confirm an estimated timeline with each quotation.

Collection & delivery

Orders can be collected from our shop. Delivery for qualifying bulk uniform orders within the UAE can be arranged, with any applicable delivery charge included in the quotation.
